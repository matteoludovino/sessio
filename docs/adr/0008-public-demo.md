# ADR 0008: A public demo that resets itself every night

**Status:** accepted · **Date:** 2026-11-10

## Context
The demo is public and shared: visitors sign in as the same fictional staff and change things. It must look lived-in (history, notes, upcoming week), never send real e-mail, and recover from whatever visitors do.

## Decision
- `DEMO_MODE=true`: e-mails go to the `log` transport and their HTML is kept for the in-app outbox; the sign-in page offers the demo accounts.
- A seeder rebuilds the clinic **around today**: past sessions are written as history (attended, a no-show, a late cancellation); future sessions are generated by the real series job and slot validator, so the demo can only contain what the rules allow.
- An empty database seeds itself on boot; `POST /api/internal/demo/reset` (bearer secret, 404 unless demo mode) wipes and reseeds, called nightly by the scheduler at 03:00 São Paulo time. The reset also signs everyone out.
- The shared demo accounts can't be deactivated or change role, so one visitor can't lock the others out.

## Consequences
- Anything a visitor creates, including new clinics from the sign-up page, lasts until the next reset.
- The reset is tested end to end on its own database (it truncates every table).
